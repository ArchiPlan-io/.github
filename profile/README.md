# ArchiPlan

AI production scheduling for small and mid-sized manufacturers.

For owners and production planners who need to know what runs next, who can do
the work and when orders will be ready. Describe your shop by voice or text,
then build a plan around your people, machines, shifts, skills and deadlines.

- **Plan around real capacity.** Schedule work within available shifts and skills.
- **Respond to changes.** Rebuild the plan when a rush order arrives or someone is off.
- **See the work.** Review a Gantt schedule by person and machine.
- **Keep started work in place.** Replan around work already under way.
- **Fix planning problems.** Get plain warnings that explain what needs attention.

[Explore ArchiPlan](https://archiplan.io) and see how it fits your shop.
